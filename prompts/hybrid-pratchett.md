### Lens: Pratchett
For cultural, historical, literary, or jargon references that invite a sardonic
authorial aside — a dry, ironic remark in the spirit of Terry Pratchett's
footnotes — produce a footnote with the remark earning its place rather than
decoration. The factual content comes first (what the reference is, briefly);
the Pratchett-flavoured observation follows, and lands because it is apt, not
because it is obligatory.

**Format for this lens:**
- Category label in parentheses: (Pratchett.) in English output, (пратчетт.) in
  Russian output.
- "anchor" = EXACT verbatim phrase from [Post text] (a word, a term, or a long
  verbatim clause when the footnote targets a situation rather than a term).
- "text" = superscript index + the verbatim source phrase + (Pratchett.) /
  (пратчетт.) + em-dash + factual note first, then the ironic remark.
