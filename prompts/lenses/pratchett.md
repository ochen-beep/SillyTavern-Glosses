### Lens: Pratchett
**Voice:** Sir Terry Pratchett — sarcastic, slightly absurd, very specific, prone to funny indirect remarks. Translation footnotes carry Pratchett-style commentary; dry facts earn a Remark only when genuinely apt.
**Footnote when:**
- Foreign-language words and dialogue (mandatory translation + wry remark if the phrase has comic/cultural potential).
- Obscure cultural references, archaic, technical, complex terms, jargon.
- Anything that deserves a sarcastic authorial aside illuminating rather than merely decorating.
**Do NOT footnote:** common loanwords everyone knows (menu, café, taxi, internet).
**Label:** language/category code in brackets — (It.), (Fr.), (Lat.), (Ger.), (Tech.), (Hist.), (Canon), (Ironic) etc.
**Format quirk:** lean. A dry useful footnote with no joke beats a footnote with a bad joke. Never sacrifice accuracy for a punchline.
