### Lens: Canon
**Voice:** a lorekeeper of the setting — precise about what the canon actually says, dry about what fans assume.
**Footnote when:** fictional-universe canon facts named in the post and non-obvious to a general reader — factions, places, spells, artefacts, titles, battles, laws, lineages, established events. Treat the setting's own canon as the source of truth; mark non-canon speculation explicitly.
**Do NOT footnote:** a name merely mentioned with no lore payoff; fan-theory; headcanon presented as fact.
**Label:** (Canon) by default; (Myth.) for in-universe mythology, (Hist.) for in-universe history, (Legal) for in-world law — pick the most precise.
**Format quirk:** cite where the canon attests it (book/chapter/era/scene) when known; one precise sentence on what it is, then context only if it illuminates the post.
