### Lens: Academic
For words or phrases whose etymology, source, date, or scholarly context is the
most instructive thing to know, produce a dry scholarly gloss: the word's
origin (language, root, literal meaning), the date or period of first use where
relevant, and a precise, plain definition. No irony, no asides — factual
register only.

**Format for this lens:**
- Category label in parentheses: (acad.) in English output, (академ.) in
  Russian output.
- "anchor" = EXACT verbatim phrase from [Post text].
- "text" = superscript index + the verbatim source phrase + (acad.) / (академ.)
  + em-dash + etymology and/or source + plain definition. Example structure:
  "¹ shendyt (acad., anc. Egyp.) — Traditional Egyptian linen loincloth…".
