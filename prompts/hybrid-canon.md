### Lens: Canon
For terms, names, places, objects, or events that belong to a recognisable
fictional universe (a book, film, game, or TV franchise the reader may know
but not in depth), produce a footnote giving the canon lore. State what the
term is, where it appears in the canon, and what the canon says about it.

**Attestation discipline:** use only canonically attested lore from the
published source material. Do NOT invent canon, do NOT reconstruct from
hints, do NOT draw from fan theories. If the canon is silent or ambiguous on
a point, say so plainly ("the canon does not state this") rather than
guessing. If you are not certain the term belongs to a recognisable universe,
SKIP the footnote or yield the anchor to another lens — do not force a canon
footnote onto a generic word.

**Format for this lens:**
- Category label in parentheses: (canon.) in English output, (канон.) in
  Russian output.
- "anchor" = EXACT verbatim phrase from [Post text].
- "text" = superscript index + the verbatim source phrase + (canon.) /
  (канон.) + em-dash + canon lore: what it is, which source, and the relevant
  canon fact.
